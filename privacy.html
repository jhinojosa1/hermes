Privacy policy

This policy covers two personal apps: Hermes and Agenda.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant and Agenda a desktop calendar widget, both used only by their owner. With the owner's permission they access the owner's own Google account (Hermes: Calendar, Gmail and Drive; Agenda: Calendar) only to carry out the owner's requests.

  • Google data is used only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own private devices.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Their use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
