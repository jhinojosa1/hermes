Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Calendar, with the owner's permission, to read, create, change and delete calendar events at the owner's request.

  • Calendar data is used only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
